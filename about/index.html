Two guys who sucked at watering.

Happy Plant started as a personal challenge: learn to build iOS apps, and fix one constant problem at home. We kept forgetting to water the plants. D’oh!

the culprits, circa 2017

1 · the problem

The plants kept dying

Mike kept forgetting to water. His girlfriend was not amused. Watering wasn’t hard. It was just boring, and boring things get forgotten.

2 · the ah-ha

Make boring fun

A reminder app sounded dull, even to us. So we gave plants names, selfies, streaks and attitude, and suddenly watering became the fun part of the day.

3 · the mission

Life changers

Apple featured us as an app that helps people stay organized at home. That’s when it clicked: our mission is turning boring activities into fun, life changers.

“Our mission is simple: turn boring activities into fun, life changers.”
