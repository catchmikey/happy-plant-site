App and Website Privacy and Cookie Policy

Happy Plant is an app created by Webodew. It is registered under SemanticJungle.

Happy Plant is an app that helps people remember to water their plants.

At the same time, we are concerned about privacy just as you should be! And so please let me say a few words about your privacy when using our app and this website.

App: Information Collection and Usage

Usage logs. In our effort to improve Happy Plant App, we gather information about how our users use the app using a third party provider called Fabric.io. The information we gather relates to stats about plants (water time, daily usage, plant names, etc.) and notifying us if the app crashed and what made it crash. The information that we request is NOT personally identifiable and is retained by us.

Crash reports. Happy Plant uses Fabric.io for crash reporting. In the event of a crash, a crash report will automatically be transmitted to Fabric.io on the next launch. We access these crash reports to fix problems with the app. These crash reports do not contain any identifying information or data that you’ve saved in the app.

Fabric.io is a Google service; its privacy policy applies to the data it processes.

Website: The Use of Cookies

The Cookie Monster loves cookies. But that doesn’t mean we all do. Heck, we sometimes need to be on diet to save our tummies. Regarding web cookies used on our site, happyplantapp.com, we are only using Google Analytics. If you’ve never heard of them, they gather anonymous site statistics about people who visit our website. It’s a great tool that allows us to understand our web visitors and provide more benefiting value to our audience. And it’s also anonymous, meaning that we never possess information that can be attributed to a particular individual.

When you visit our site, you are anonymous.

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us. We’ll make sure that all issues will be addressed.

Information Collection

If you use HappyPlantApp.com, you have the option to submit inquiries. Your information such as name, website, email address and phone number may be submitted. If so, these will go directly to and only to emails owned by Webodew so that Webodew can communicate back to you.

We do not share information.

Information Usage

Webodew uses collected information to communicate with our website users, market research and provide continued product and service improvement.

The information Webodew collects is not shared with anyone. No organizations, non-Webodew individuals. We do not sell information to others for commercial purposes.

It may be necessary to share information in order to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the physical safety of any person, violations of Terms of Service, or as otherwise required by law. In such an event, you will be notified.

If one day Webodew is acquired by or merged with another company, there will be a transfer of information and requirement for users to comply with a different privacy policy. If an acquisition does occur, Webodew will notify every client and user before the transfer is made.
